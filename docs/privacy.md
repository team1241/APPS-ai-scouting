# Privacy boundaries and release requirements

Issue #19. Reviewed 2026-11-07. This policy covers Canadian teams, high-school-age scouts using Telegram, and adult organization administrators. It does not authorize younger audiences, public enrollment, cross-organization sharing, or a public scouting-write API.

## Implementation status

The backend exposes Telegram intake, health checks and Clerk-session-authenticated organization discovery. The Next.js app provides sign-in and approved-admin organization selection. Operators record adult approval before issuing an invite-only Clerk invitation, then explicitly link the verified subject to the approved existing member. No Telegram interaction provisions a Clerk account. Unkey management and data exports remain future surfaces; the requirements below are mandatory contracts for them. Mastra observability retains content-free, allowlisted traces locally and, when configured, in Mastra Platform. Durable workflow snapshots still contain notes and results for recovery, and are private operational data.

## Adult administration

Web copy: “Team administration is for approved adults only. Scouts submit notes through Telegram. Ask your team’s adult lead for access.” Adult means at least 18 AND the age of majority in the administrator's province or territory. Do not request birthdays, identity documents, school details, demographics, profile names, photos, or phone numbers.

Before issuing an invitation, an existing authorized organization lead attests: “I confirm this person is an adult under the rule above, is an existing approved administrator of this team, and is authorized to manage its access.” Record organization ID, existing member ID, approver member ID, policy version, and approval time in a private approval record. Do not infer eligibility from a Telegram role, email domain, possession of an invitation, or an identity-provider login. Bootstrap approval must come from the team's responsible adult lead through the operator, outside public signup. Recheck approval and current admin membership on enrollment and every administrative authorization; fail closed when either is absent or revoked.

Disable public Clerk signup and automatic account provisioning/linking from Telegram. Validate the invitation and approval before creating an identity-provider account; a post-signup denial alone is insufficient. Bind the verified provider subject to the approved existing member through a server-side, single-use enrollment flow. Do not accept client-supplied member/organization mappings or link accounts by matching email alone. Removing membership or approval revokes sessions and API keys. Do not ship web enrollment until this path has integration tests for scouts, unapproved adults, revoked admins, wrong organizations, replayed invitations, and direct signup bypasses.

If a minor accidentally registers: immediately disable access, revoke sessions and keys, stop further synchronization, and contact the organization's responsible adult privately. Remove the accidental Clerk account, local auth link, unnecessary profile/contact data, and associated Unkey records; request vendor deletion where needed. Preserve only a restricted incident record with dates, actions, and opaque reference, not copied profiles or age evidence. Review any disclosures and required notifications with the responsible privacy lead. Do not delete legitimate Telegram scouting records automatically; handle those through the request procedure below.

## Data API contract

Every request must validate a live key and bind the database tenant to its authorized organization; reject client overrides and cross-organization IDs. Reads only. Do not expose raw messages, revisions/history, computed statistics, scout/member/Telegram IDs, account details, sessions, delivery IDs, internal revision IDs, provenance, validation payloads, or timestamps that identify individual submissions. Do not reuse internal repository rows or spread them into responses.

Build an explicit output allowlist for event/season, team, match, specification version and requested extracted scouting fields. Select each extracted key against the authorized published specification; do not export arbitrary nested `values` or metadata. Preserve requested extracted notes. They are free text and may contain incidental personal information; exports are **not anonymous**. Apply authorization before projection; a serializer is not a tenant boundary. Require regression tests with extra identifying fields at every nesting level and cross-tenant requests before enabling the endpoint. No scouting-data browser is part of this release.

## Retention and requests

These are release operating limits, not an implemented automatic purge job. The operator must configure and verify cleanup before release, including foreign keys and durable workflow storage. Never run an unreviewed production purge.

| Data                                                                        | Maximum routine retention                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Raw notes, linked sessions/snapshots, revisions, flags and delivery content | Season end plus 90 days; remove earlier on a validated deletion request where possible.                      |
| Member/channel/auth links and adult approvals                               | While membership is active; revoke immediately on removal and delete within 30 days.                         |
| Expired/revoked invitations and intake rejection identifiers                | 30 days.                                                                                                     |
| Content-free essential security/incident records and logs                   | 30 days, unless a documented incident/legal hold requires a specific longer period with review date.         |
| Backups                                                                     | 30 days after live deletion; verify provider settings and prevent deleted data from returning after restore. |

The team's responsible adult lead is the initial privacy contact; the service operator handles database and vendor work. Publish that lead's working contact with the notice before enrollment. Accept access, correction, and deletion requests from scouts or their authorized parent/guardian via that contact. Verify using existing team relationships or authenticated channels, collecting no new identity documents by default. Acknowledge within seven days and target completion within 30 days, explaining delays or narrowly documented legal holds.

Locate records tenant-by-tenant using internal member/channel links. Revoke access first when requested. Include raw entries, revisions/provenance, flags, participation, invitations, outbound payloads, Mastra snapshots and traces in the review; deleting the member row alone is insufficient. Correct incidental personal information in text and downstream copies, not just a displayed result. Coordinate Clerk/Unkey/model/hosting deletion where applicable and report completion and remaining backup expiry to the requester. Keep only a minimal request/action record. After restore, reapply deletions before reopening access. Telegram and recipients' downloaded copies require separate requests; explain this limitation.

## Vendor review

Reviewed public documentation on 2026-11-07; these findings are not a legal-compliance certification. The operator must confirm the actual subscription, DPA, subprocessors, retention, international processing, and applicable youth requirements before launch.

- [Clerk standard terms](https://clerk.com/legal/standard-terms) place identity and authorized-user access responsibilities on the customer. No explicit minor-specific clause was found in that page. [Clerk's DPA](https://clerk.com/legal/dpa) distinguishes account information from customer data and permits processing where its facilities/subprocessors operate. [Privacy policy](https://clerk.com/legal/privacy) directs end-user customer-data questions to the customer. Do not interpret this as approval to enroll minors; confirm applicable contract requirements before enabling Clerk.
- [Custom OIDC configuration](https://clerk.com/docs/guides/configure/auth-strategies/social-connections/custom-provider) supports selective claim mapping. If Telegram OIDC is later selected, [Telegram login documentation](https://core.telegram.org/bots/telegram-login) describes optional phone and messaging permissions. Request only `openid` initially; do not request `profile`, phone, contacts, or messaging access. Any provider-required additional claim needs a documented necessity review. Existing bot access does not require web login.
- [Unkey terms](https://www.unkey.com/policies/terms) require users of its site to be at least 18; [privacy policy](https://www.unkey.com/policies/privacy) describes its under-13 handling. Keep vendor administration adult-only and send no scout personal information. Confirm the service agreement applies to the selected API product. The issue's old billing documentation URL could not be retrieved; [current pricing](https://www.unkey.com/pricing) describes usage-based plans and active-key billing, so the historical 1,000-key/150,000-operation free allowance is not a verified current budget assumption.
